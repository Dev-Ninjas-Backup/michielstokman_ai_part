Title:

Letting the Ego Teach You – A Guided Meditation for True Freedom

A Guided Meditation by Emma

April 2025

Age: 42 (almost 43)

Gender & Sexual orientation: Woman, heterosexual

Country / City: France / Nice

Life phase: Deep inner transformation after years of spiritual study and a pivotal
moment in her intimate relationship

Relationship status & main dynamic: In a committed relationship that has grown
profoundly since she stopped reacting from ego

Core emotional state right now: Quiet freedom mixed with tender appreciation for the
ego’s role

Specific trigger / situation: Realizing that the ego is not the enemy but a teacher that
needs to be understood, contained, and appreciated rather than destroyed

Deepest desire: To live in lasting liberation while staying fully human and relational

Desired High Intensity Toggle: Off

Longer Meditation (≈ 1580 words with pauses – spoken length approx. 11 minutes)



Take a comfortable seat or lie down if that feels better for you right now.

Gently close your eyes, or soften your gaze if you prefer to keep them open.

[6s]

Let your shoulders drop away from your ears.

Let your jaw soften.

--- PAGE 2 ---
And simply notice the weight of your body resting on the surface beneath you.

[10s]

Begin with a slow, natural breath.

Feel the cool air entering through your nostrils… and the warmer air leaving through
your mouth.

Nothing to fix. Nothing to achieve. Just breathing.

[6s]

You are here.

In this moment.

In this body.

In this life.

And today we are not here to fight any part of you.

We are here to meet, to understand, and to gently transform the relationship with the
one part that has felt like the biggest obstacle for so long — your ego.

Bring your attention softly into the centre of your chest.

Feel the gentle rise and fall as you breathe.

And if it feels right for you, place one hand there, over your heart.

[8s]

For many years you believed the enemy was outside — the world, other people,
circumstances, even the words of your partner.

But deeper inside, you began to see something else.

The real source of struggle was the protective mask you had built.

The ego.

The small “I” that needed to be right, to be safe, to be seen, to defend itself.

And now, in this quiet space, you are invited to look at it differently.

Take a deeper breath in… and as you exhale, allow these words to land gently inside
you:

“And even my ego is not an enemy.”

--- PAGE 3 ---
[10s]

It is just there.

As a teacher.

As a way to grow.

To learn.

To interact with your world.

It is an essential part of you.

You simply needed to learn how it works.

Not to give it too much power.

To contain it.

To appreciate it.

Feel those words move through your body like warm light.

Notice where they land — perhaps in your chest, your belly, your throat, or
somewhere deeper.

Let the sensation be exactly as it is. No need to change anything.

[10s]

Now bring to mind a moment when the ego used to take the wheel.

Perhaps a time when you felt personally attacked.

When old pain rose quickly and you wanted to strike back, defend, prove your worth.

See that moment without judgment.

Just observe it like a movie playing at a distance.

[8s]

Notice the physical sensations that used to come with it — the tightening in your
stomach, the heat in your face, the racing thoughts.

And now, while still holding that memory lightly, breathe into those places.

Softly. Kindly.

[6s]

--- PAGE 4 ---
And what if you can also notice something new today?

What if the ego is not something to kill or destroy, but something to understand?

A loyal protector that once kept you safe in the only way it knew how.

A teacher that showed you where you still believed you were small, separate, or not
enough.

Feel the shift.

From fighting… to meeting.

From rejecting… to containing.

From fearing… to appreciating.

[10s]

Let a soft warmth spread through your body as you breathe.

With every inhale you welcome the ego as part of your human experience.

With every exhale you gently contain it — you give it its proper place, not the driver’s
seat, but a seat beside you.

[8s]

Now imagine sitting on that mountain again — the one where you spent so many
hours reading the ancient teachings.

The Tao, the Buddha, Jesus, Plato, the Gita.

All of them pointing in the same direction.

Not to destroy the self, but to stop being completely identified with the small self.

And today you understand something even deeper.

You do not need to annihilate the ego to be free.

You only need to stop handing it all the power.

You can watch it.

You can smile at it when it starts its old stories.

You can say, “Thank you for trying to protect me… I’ve got this now.”

And then return to the vast, quiet awareness that you truly are — the ocean, not the
ripple.

--- PAGE 5 ---
Feel that spaciousness opening around you now.

Your body may stay the same, yet inside there is more room.

More breath.

More presence.

More love that does not need to defend itself.

[10s]

Bring to mind your partner again.

Recall that moment when he spoke from his pain and, instead of reacting from your
ego, you met him with love.

Feel how different that response was.

How light.

How connective.

That shift did not come from destroying the ego.

It came from seeing it clearly… and choosing not to let it drive.

Let gratitude rise for that moment.

Gratitude for the ego that showed you where you were still attached.

Gratitude for the practice that allowed you to respond from a deeper place.

[8s]

Now place both hands gently on your body — wherever it feels most soothing.

One on your heart, one on your belly perhaps.

And speak silently or softly to yourself, in the spirit of gentle self-love:

“I see you, ego.

Thank you for all the ways you have tried to keep me safe.

I no longer need to fight you.

I no longer need to give you the steering wheel.

You can stay.

But I am learning to contain you with kindness and wisdom.

--- PAGE 6 ---
I appreciate you as my teacher.

And I am free to live as the ocean that I am.”

[10s]

Stay here as long as you like.

Feeling the balance between the human part that still has stories and fears… and the
vast awareness that holds everything without being touched by it.

This is the quiet liberation you have been moving toward.

Not the absence of ego, but the end of identification with it.

Not rejection, but wise appreciation and gentle containment.

When you are ready, take a few deeper breaths.

Feel your feet on the ground or your body on the surface.

Bring a soft smile to your face — even if just inside.

And whenever the ego begins to speak loudly again during your day, you can return
to this simple remembering:

“It is just there… as a teacher… and I know how to contain it now.”

You are safe.

You are learning.

You are already freer than you were.

Whenever you feel ready, you can slowly open your eyes and carry this gentle,
spacious relationship with yourself into the rest of your day.

Thank you for sitting with me.

You are doing beautiful work.







Guided Meditation: Listening to Your Body – Temple of Health

From Jack, Austria, 45

Hello, I’m Jack. I’m 45 and I live in Austria.

--- PAGE 7 ---
For a long time I lived mostly in my head — worrying, judging, pushing.

I forgot to really listen to my body. Today I invite you to do what I learned: come back
to your body with love and respect. Your body is your temple. It wants to be healthy,
strong, and full of energy.

Find a comfortable position. Sit or lie down.

Gently close your eyes.

Take three slow, deep breaths with me…

Inhaling peace… exhaling tension.

Let your shoulders drop. Let your jaw soften. Let your whole body become heavy and
relaxed.

Now simply breathe naturally. Come to rest. Come to peace.

Your head is often loud and stuck in small thoughts — planning, worrying, judging.

Right now, give your body a real chance.

Move your attention away from the mind and gently drop it into your body.

Listen to your body – speak to each part from your heart

Place one hand softly on your heart. Feel its steady, warm beat.

From this loving center, speak silently or quietly to your body parts. Don’t let the head
answer — listen with your heart.

Ask your heart:

“Heart, how are you feeling today? What do you need from me?”

…Listen. Feel its answer in sensations, warmth, rhythm.

Ask your back:

“Dear back, how are you? Where do you carry tension? What would help you feel
better?”

…Listen softly.

Ask your legs:

“Legs, thank you for carrying me every day. How do you feel? Do you need more rest,
more movement, or simply more love?”

…Listen.

--- PAGE 8 ---
Ask your muscles and joints:

“Muscles, shoulders, knees, arms… I see you. I feel you. What do you want to tell
me?”

…Stay open and receive their honest message.

Your body is speaking all the time. When you listen from the heart instead of the head,
it answers with truth and gentleness.

Nourish and celebrate your body

Now imagine feeding your body with good, nourishing food. See yourself choosing
fresh, vibrant meals that make every cell smile.

Say to your body, from the heart:

“You are beautiful.”

Repeat it many times — 50 times a day, or even better, 250 times a day.

“I love you. You are beautiful. You are my temple.”

Don’t let the destructive judgements of popular culture, social media, or old voices
tell you otherwise.

Your body is perfect exactly as it is right now. It is strong. It is alive. It is yours.

Releasing pain and welcoming new energy

If there is any pain, tension, or tiredness in your body, speak to it kindly:

“I see you. I accept you. You can let go now.”

Feel the pain softening, loosening, flowing out with every exhale.

Now visualize a beautiful beam of healing light.

One ray of warm golden light rises from the earth, through the soles of your feet,
filling your legs, your hips, your spine, all the way up.

Another ray of bright, clear light descends from the sky, through the top of your head,
flowing down through your body.

Both beams meet in your heart center, mixing into a powerful, loving energy.

Say silently:

“I accept this new energy completely. My body is ready.”

“Thank you, earth. Thank you, sky. Thank you, dear body.”

--- PAGE 9 ---
Feel this light nourishing every cell, every organ, every muscle.

Your body drinks it in. It becomes stronger, lighter, more vibrant with every breath.

Closing

Rest for a moment in this beautiful state. Your body is listening. Your body is grateful.
Your body is healing and renewing itself right now.

Slowly bring gentle movement back to your fingers and toes.

When you are ready, open your eyes.

Carry this loving attention with you today.

Keep listening to your body. Keep telling it “You are beautiful” many times.

Feed it well. Let old pain go. Accept new energy.

Your body is your temple.

Treat it with love, and it will respond with health, strength, and joy.

You deserve to feel good in your body.

You already are healing.

Namaste, from Jack in Austria.

Guided Meditation: Center of Peace – Opening the Third Eye

Find a comfortable seat or lying position. Gently close your eyes.

Take three slow, deep breaths… inhaling calm… exhaling tension.

Let your body soften. Let your breath become natural and easy.

Bring your attention to the very center of your chest — your heart-space, your core of
inner peace.

This is your home. This is your starting point. This is where everything begins and
returns.

Feel a warm, steady light glowing here. In this state of being, you are safe. You are
whole. Everything is okay. You are both the observer and the player. You simply are.

Rest here for a few moments… breathing into this center of love and trust… feeling
the quiet peace that is always available to you.

First small lesson – return to center – open to third eye

Imagine you are caught in the day: thoughts racing, stress rising, emotions pulling
you left and right. Worry about tomorrow, regret about yesterday, irritation in the
present. Your mind feels heavy, entangled.

Notice the pull… and softly say inside: “Go back.”

Return gently to your center of peace. Feel the warm light grow stronger. The storm of
thoughts and emotions is still there, but you are no longer lost inside it. You are the
calm space holding it all.

From this peaceful center, now allow your attention to rise naturally to the space
between your eyebrows — your third eye.

Feel a gentle violet or indigo light blooming here. This is your inner vision, your
expanded awareness.

From your center of peace, you have full access to this gift.

Now, with the third eye open, you can see differently.

You rise lightly… following a bird gliding high above the trees. You feel the wind
under imaginary wings. You look down at the earth from above — cities, rivers,
mountains — all small and beautiful, without any emotional charge.

--- PAGE 14 ---
You are simply seeing. You are the clear perspective.

You can travel to the past or future like turning pages in a book — observing without
being pulled in. No fear, no excitement, just pure seeing.

You realize: you are not only the peaceful center… you are also this vast third-eye
awareness. You can enjoy this perspective as part of you. It is free, light, and deeply
peaceful at the same time.

Enjoy this expanded view for a moment… then softly bring your awareness back
down through the center of peace, resting there again. You are home.

Second small lesson – return to center – embrace the third eye

Now picture yourself deeply involved in someone else’s energy. Maybe you feel their
worries, their thoughts, their emotions mixing with yours. It feels heavy, confusing.
You lose your own ground.

Notice the entanglement… and whisper: “Go back.”

Return to your warm center of inner peace. Feel the light steady and clear once more.
The external energies are still present, but they no longer overwhelm you. You hold
your own calm space.

From this center, rise again to your third eye.

Let the indigo light expand. Now you can gently travel into the minds of others if you
wish — not to interfere, but to understand with compassion and clarity. You see their
thoughts like clouds passing. You observe without judgment.

You can also travel upward into higher light, or downward into the quiet depth of the
earth — always from a place of freedom.

You smile softly as you realize: “I am also this third eye. I can choose this perspective
whenever I want.”

It feels liberating. You enjoy being the bird, the watcher from above, the clear seer
who is not entangled. This is part of you — a beautiful, natural ability that grows from
your peaceful core.

Rest in this third-eye perspective for a little while… feeling how light and enjoyable it
is… then gently return through your center of peace, letting the warm light embrace
you again.

Third small lesson – return to center – integrate third eye

One more scene arises: you are lost in strong emotions — anger, sadness, or
overwhelming desire. The mind spins stories, the body feels tight. You forget who you
really are.

--- PAGE 15 ---
Feel the intensity… and kindly say: “Go back to center.”

Return fully to the glowing light in your chest. Peace flows in like a gentle river. The
emotions are still moving, but you are the space that holds them. You are safe. You
are love and trust.

From this steady center, invite your third eye to open once more.

Now you can look at the same emotions from the third-eye view — like watching a
movie on a screen. You see the story without being trapped in it. You can zoom out,
follow the energy like a bird soaring over a stormy sea, knowing the sky is always
vast and clear.

You realize with joy: you are the peaceful center and the third eye. You can move
between them freely. You can enjoy the wide, emotion-free perspective whenever you
choose. It is not separate from you — it is you, expanded.

Breathe in that beautiful integration… feeling both the deep peace of your core and
the clear freedom of your third eye.

Closing

Take a few moments to rest in your center of inner peace. Know that from here you
always have access to your third eye — the gift of clear seeing, lucid awareness, and
vast perspective.

You can return anytime: when stress pulls you, when emotions entangle you, when
thoughts spin too fast. Simply go back to center… then rise to third eye if you wish…
and enjoy being the bird, the watcher, the seer.

Slowly wiggle your fingers and toes. When you feel ready, open your eyes.

Carry this warm center and this open third eye with you through your day.

You are peace. You are clear vision. You are free to enjoy both.

Whenever life pulls you away, the marker is always there:

“Go back to center… then open to your third eye.”

You are already whole.

You are already home.

Namaste.





Guided Meditation: – Returning to Your State of Being

--- PAGE 16 ---
Find a quiet, comfortable place. Sit or lie down in a way that feels easy for your body.
Gently close your eyes. Take three slow, deep breaths… inhaling peace… exhaling
anything that feels heavy. Let your shoulders soften, your jaw relax, your breath settle
into its own natural rhythm.

Now, imagine a simple marker appears before you – a glowing signpost on the path of
life. It reads:
 “Liberation begins here – in the State of Being.”
 This is your starting point. This is your return point. This is your home.

Feel yourself walking toward the very center of your chest… your heart-space… your
core soul. Here lives a warm, steady light – the center of peace, the center of love and
trust. Nothing needs to be fixed. Nothing needs to be added. You are already whole. In
this state of being, everything grows naturally. Everything is okay. Everything unfolds
exactly as it should. You are both the observer and the player at once. You simply
enjoy what is. You are what is.

First small lesson – and return to the core
 Picture an ordinary day. You are rushing, late for something important. Traffic slows,
plans slip, irritation rises like a sudden storm. Anger bubbles up. “Why is this
happening to me?” Your mind races into blame and frustration.
 In that moment you feel the pull… and you remember the marker.
 You gently place one hand on your heart and whisper inside: “Go back.”
 You return to your center core… the state of love and trust. The storm is still there,
but you are no longer inside it. You are the calm sky watching the clouds pass. From
this place everything is okay again. Peace flows through you like warm light. You
breathe, and the irritation loses its grip. You smile softly, because you remember: true
happiness never depended on the traffic clearing. It lives right here, in your state of
being.

Feel that peace deepen now… breathe into the glowing center… you are home.

Second small lesson – and return to the core
 Now imagine you are chasing a big dream. Money, success, recognition. You keep
thinking, “When I get there… then I will finally be happy.” You worry about the future.
What if it doesn’t happen? What if you are not enough? Expectations tighten around
your chest like a rope.
 Again, you notice the pull… and you see the glowing marker: “Liberation begins
here.”
 You softly say, “Go back.”
 You step back into your center core… the state of peace and love. Suddenly the
future feels light. You realize: no amount of wealth, no achievement, no outer success
can ever fill you the way this inner state already does. From here, you can still move
toward your dreams – but you move from fullness, not from lack. Everything grows
naturally. You are already blossoming. You are already enough. True self-fulfillment
was never waiting somewhere else. It is right here, in your core soul.

Breathe deeply into that glowing center… let the warmth spread through your whole
body… you are home.

--- PAGE 17 ---
Third small lesson – and return to the core
 One more picture arises. You look at your life and compare. You see beautiful people,
big houses, perfect relationships, exciting travels. A quiet voice whispers, “If only I
had that… then I would feel complete.” A small ache of longing or envy appears.
 You feel the gentle pull away from yourself… and once more the marker shines:
“Liberation begins here – in the State of Being.”
 You smile and say, “Go back.”
 You return to your center core… the state of love and trust. In this place, beauty is
not something you need to own – it is already flowing through you. Friends, houses,
cars, adventures… they are lovely gifts, but they are not the source. The source is
you, exactly as you are. In this state you can enjoy everything just as it is. You can
love deeply without needing anything back. You are the observer and the player at
once. True happiness, true blossoming, true freedom always starts and always lives
right here – in your core soul.

Rest in that center now… feel the steady light glowing brighter… nothing outside can
ever take this away.

Closing
 Take a moment to thank yourself for returning again and again. This state of being is
always here for you – in traffic, in dreams, in comparison, in joy, in pain. Whenever life
pulls you out, the marker is waiting. Simply place your hand on your heart and
whisper: “Go back… to love… to trust… to peace.”

Slowly begin to move your fingers and toes. When you are ready, open your eyes.
Carry this glowing center with you into the rest of your day. You are the state of being.
You are already liberated. Everything is okay. You are home.

Whenever you need it, come back to this meditation. The marker is always shining.
 The core is always waiting.
 You are already free.

Namaste.
